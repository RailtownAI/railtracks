Evaluators are one of the three core components in **Railtracks**'s evaluation framework. They are the classes responsible for running evaluations over a dataset and producing structured results.

Currently we have the following abstractions for Evaluators:

- [ToolUseEvaluator](https://docs.railtracks.org/evaluations/evaluators/tool_use_evaluator/index.md)
- [LLMInferenceEvaluator](https://docs.railtracks.org/evaluations/evaluators/llm_inference_evaluator/index.md)
- [JudgeEvaluator](https://docs.railtracks.org/evaluations/evaluators/judge_evaluator/index.md)
